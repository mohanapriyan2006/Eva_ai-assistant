## 👋 What Makes Eva Special?

Imagine having a friend who's always available, never judges your questions, and can talk to you just like a real person. That's Eva! Whether you're feeling chatty and want to type, or you're hands-deep in cooking and need to ask something out loud – Eva's got you covered.

### � **Eva's Superpowers:**

🗣️ **She Actually Listens to You** – No more typing when your hands are full! Just talk to Eva like you would to a friend, and she'll understand every word.

🧠 **Smart as a Whip** – Powered by advanced AI that remembers context and gives you responses that actually make sense (unlike some people we know! 😉).

⚡ **Lightning Fast** – Built with React.js because nobody has time to wait around for slow apps.

🎤 **Speaks Your Language** – Uses cutting-edge voice tech to chat with you naturally – it's like having a conversation with a really smart friend.

🎨 **Easy on the Eyes** – Designed with Tailwind CSS to look absolutely gorgeous on any device you're using.

## � The Cool Tech Behind Eva

*Ever wondered what makes Eva tick? Here's the secret sauce:*

- **🎨 Frontend Magic:** React.js (because we love things that just *work*)
- **✨ Style Wizard:** Tailwind CSS (making everything look effortlessly beautiful)
- **🧠 Brain Power:** Gemini AI (the genius behind Eva's smart responses)
- **🎙️ Voice Magic:** Web Speech API (for those "OMG, she actually understood me!" moments)
